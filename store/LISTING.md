# Google Play listing draft

**Title:** Splitty: Split Bills & Trips
**Short (80):** Split bills and group trips fairly. Private, offline, no sign-up.
**Full:** Splitty makes sharing costs painless. Create a group, add expenses, and see exactly who owes whom, with debts simplified to the fewest payments. Split equally, by exact amounts, percentages or shares. Attach receipt photos, use any currency per expense, set recurring bills, search, and export to CSV. Everything is stored on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking.
**Category:** Finance (or Tools). **Privacy policy:** https://splitty.tenkodigital.com/privacy.html
**Data safety:** collects no data, shares no data; camera and photo access only to attach receipts, stored on device.
**Assets:** play-icon-512.png, feature-graphic.png (1024x500), screenshots in this folder. Upload: release/splitty.aab.
**Blocker:** needs a Play Console developer account.
