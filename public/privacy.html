Splitty privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Splitty is a bill-splitting app by Tenko Digital. It is built to keep your data on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. We run no servers for Splitty, have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. We never receive your groups, names, expenses or receipts.

What is stored on your device

  • Groups, people, expenses and balances, in a local SQLite database.
  • Receipt photos you scan or pick, saved in the app's private storage (on the web version, inside your browser's storage).

Deleting a group or expense removes its data. Uninstalling the app (or clearing site data in the browser) removes everything.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you tap Scan, to photograph a receipt.
  • Photo library: only when you tap From gallery, to attach an existing picture. Splitty only receives the pictures you select.

Sharing

Data leaves your device only when you export a CSV and share it yourself using your phone's share sheet.

Children

Splitty does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
