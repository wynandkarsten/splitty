Install Splitty

Free, and it takes about a minute. Pick your phone.

🤖

Android

Download the app file and open it.

Download for Android

  1. Tap the button and open the downloaded splitty.apk.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser (one-time).
  3. Tap Install, then open Splitty.

🍎

iPhone

Add it to your Home Screen. It then behaves like an app.

  1. Open splitty.tenkodigital.com/app in Safari.
  2. Tap the menu button at the bottom of Safari (three lines, next to the back and forward arrows; on some iPhones it's a square with an arrow up, labelled Share).
  3. Scroll the list, choose Add to Home Screen (it can be under View More), then Add.

Open in Safari

💻

Browser

Nothing to install. Works on any phone or computer, and keeps your data in that browser.

Open Splitty

Chrome and Edge also offer "Install app" in the address bar menu.

Is it safe?

The Android file is signed by Tenko Digital and installs outside the Play Store, which is why Android shows a one-time prompt. Splitty has no account, no ads and no trackers. Read the privacy policy. Already installed? Settings, then Check for updates.
